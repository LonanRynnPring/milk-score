How the score is poured

Every card carries an EDHREC rank: the most-played card in Commander is rank 1, and the ladder runs out past 32,000. Rank is a running order rather than a play rate, so the gap between rank 20 and rank 120 matters far more than the gap between 20,000 and 20,100. A fractional exponent compresses the crowded top of the ladder and spreads out the long tail:

card milk = 100 × (rank ÷ 32,344) ^ 0.4 · a card with no rank at all = 100 deck milk = the average card milk, weighted by copies

Basic lands — snow basics and Wastes included — never enter the average; a deck is not spicier for running eleven Forests instead of fourteen. Everything else counts, commander included. A card so rarely played that EDHREC has never ranked it takes the full 100.
